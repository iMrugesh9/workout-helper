Dumbbell Back Variations

01

Middle Back Dumbbell Row

3 × 10–12 reps
Middle BackLatsRhomboids
  • Hinge forward at the hips with a flat back
  • Pull dumbbells toward hips targeting middle back

Why: Builds thickness and strength in the middle back region.

02

Upper Back Dumbbell Row

3 × 10–12 reps
Upper BackRear DeltsTraps
  • Flare elbows outward as you pull
  • Keep upper body stable and squeeze shoulder blades

Why: Targets the upper back and rear shoulders for posture and width.

03

Lower Lat Dumbbell Row

3 × 10–12 reps
Lower LatsLats
  • Keep elbows tight to your sides
  • Drive elbows back and down toward pockets

Why: Isolates and develops the lower lat fibers.

04

Middle Traps Dumbbell Shrug Row

3 × 10–12 reps
Middle TrapsUpper Back
  • Maintain controlled hinge position
  • Focus on retracting scapula at the top

Why: Strengthens middle traps and scapular retractors.

Trainer Tips

  • Vary your grip and elbow angle to target different areas of the back.
  • Keep your core braced and spine neutral throughout all row variations.
