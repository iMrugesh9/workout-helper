Full Body Dumbbell Workout

A simple yet effective full body routine designed to train every major muscle group using a pair of dumbbells. Perfect for building muscle and strength efficiently at home.

Exercise Flow

Check off each exercise as you complete sets. Rest timer at the bottom.
01

Lateral Raises

3 × 12 reps
ShouldersLateral Delts
  • Keep a slight bend in your elbows
  • Lead with your elbows and raise weights to shoulder height

Why: Targets the lateral deltoids to build shoulder width and definition.

02

Floor Presses

3 × 10 reps
ChestTricepsShoulders
  • Keep your lower back pressed flat into the floor
  • Lower elbows to a 45-degree angle relative to your torso

Why: Develops pressing strength and upper body muscle mass while protecting the shoulders.

03

Single Arm Overhead Extensions

3 × 12 reps per arm
Triceps
  • Keep your upper arm close to your ear and stationary
  • Lower the dumbbell behind your head for a full stretch

Why: Isolates the triceps for enhanced arm size and pressing power.

04

Bent Over Rows

3 × 10 reps
BackBicepsRear Delts
  • Maintain a flat back and hinge at the hips
  • Pull the dumbbells toward your hips, squeezing your shoulder blades together

Why: Strengthens the entire posterior chain and improves posture.

05

Dumbbell Curls

3 × 12 reps
Biceps
  • Keep your elbows locked by your sides
  • Supinate your wrists as you lift for maximum contraction

Why: Builds bicep peak and arm hypertrophy.

06

Forward Lunges

3 × 10 reps per leg
QuadricepsGlutesHamstrings
  • Take a controlled step forward and lower your back knee gently toward the floor
  • Keep your torso upright and front knee tracking over your toes

Why: Enhances single-leg stability and lower body muscular endurance.

07

Dumbbell Crunches

3 × 15 reps
CoreAbdominals
  • Hold the dumbbell securely against your chest
  • Exhale as you crunch your ribs toward your pelvis, keeping lower back down

Why: Strengthens the abdominal wall and core stability with added resistance.

Trainer Tips

  • Choose a dumbbell weight that challenges you on the final 2-3 reps of each set.
  • Rest for 60-90 seconds between exercises to maintain good form.
