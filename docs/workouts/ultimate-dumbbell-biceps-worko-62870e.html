Seated Dumbbell Bicep Curl

3 × 10–12 reps
Biceps BrachiiBrachialisForearms
  • Sit upright on the bench with feet flat on the floor
  • Keep elbows tucked close to your torso
  • Rotate your palms upward (supinate) as you lift and squeeze at the top

Why: Effectively isolates the biceps overall to increase peak height, arm thickness, and grip strength.

Trainer Tips

  • Control the lowering phase (eccentric motion) for 2 to 3 seconds to maximize muscle growth.
  • Avoid using shoulder or torso swing to keep complete tension on the biceps.
